V. NOTES
PIPELINE
  1. Universe: 372 equities after removing 34 funds and 11 bonds; bottom 20% by 20-day median turnover dropped, recomputed daily.
  2. Features: 19 point-in-time signals in six families (momentum, trend, volatility, volume, relative strength, regime).
  3. Target: cross-sectional rank of 5-day excess return over the equal-weight market, entry at next open.
  4. Models: an unfitted five-factor scorecard as baseline; LightGBM regression on the rank target.
  5. Validation: expanding walk-forward; training rows whose label window reaches the test block are purged.
  6. Explanations: TreeSHAP per stock; rationale text is built only from those attributions.
LIMITATIONS
  1. 60 trading days in one falling market. Every metric here has wide error bars.
  2. Cost assumptions (35 bps commission + 10 bps slippage per side) are estimates; set yours in config.py.
  3. No dividends, rights or sector data yet. Adjusted prices are taken as given from StockNow.
  4. 151 limit-like daily moves and 160 flat bars in the data; fills near circuit breakers are uncertain.
  5. Not investment advice. This is a research tool.
